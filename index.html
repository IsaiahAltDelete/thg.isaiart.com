How to Play

24 tributes from 12 districts fight until one remains. The simulation runs itself — sit back and watch the carnage.

  • Start / Pause / Resume / Restart — control the simulation.
  • Speed — how often events fire (1 second to 1 minute).
  • Arenas / Random — change the battlefield and its backdrop.
  • Alliances — see who's teamed up (they betray each other near the end).
  • Victors — past winners, saved on your device.
  • Settings — tweak base health, hunger and pace.
  • Click any tribute card for detailed stats.

May the odds be ever in your favor.
