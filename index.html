How it works

Twenty-four enter

One boy and one girl from each of the twelve districts are reaped. The Games run on their own; you watch the feed until one tribute is left.

Training score
The number on each tribute, 1 to 12, from their strength, agility, wits and stealth.
Bars
The light bar is health, the bronze one hunger. Health turns ember when it runs low.
Cannons
Each death fires a cannon in the feed. At nightfall the anthem plays and the sky shows that day's fallen.
Alliances
Tributes team up, and betray each other. When five remain, every alliance breaks.
Arenas
Pick one from Arenas, or press Shuffle beside the arena name.
