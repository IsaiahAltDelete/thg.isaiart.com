How to Play

24 adventurers — every race and class — are dropped into a deadly realm. Last one standing is crowned champion.

  • Start / Pause / Resume / Restart — control the simulation.
  • Speed — how often events fire.
  • Realms / Random — change the battlefield.
  • Spellcasters spend mana to cast Fireball, Lightning Bolt and more.
  • Monsters roam the realm and attack the unwary.
  • Parties form and betray each other near the end.
  • Click any adventurer for full character stats.
